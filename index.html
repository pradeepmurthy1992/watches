Vintage & Luxury

History and haute horlogerie

Vintage

Vintage watches are bought for design, history and patina. In India, HMT is the heart of it. HMT began making watches in the early 1960s with technical help from Citizen of Japan, and models like the Janata, Pilot and Kohinoor became household names. HMT-branded watches are still sold today, though the original factories closed around 2016, which is a big reason older pieces are collectible.

Before you buy vintage

  • Does the crown wind and set smoothly? Do hands move on all three positions?
  • Is the dial original (clean text and lume) or redialled?
  • Crystal cracks, rust inside the case, or moisture marks are red flags
  • Ask when it was last serviced, and whether parts are available
  • Beware of 'Franken-watches' built from mixed parts

Luxury

What you pay for in a luxury watch: the movement and its hand finishing, materials (904L steel, gold, ceramic, sapphire), brand heritage, and scarcity. Resale value matters too: some models hold value well; many do not.
